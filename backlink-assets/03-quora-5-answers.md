**Find this question on Quora and paste:**

```
Getting more Google reviews comes down to one thing: asking at the 
right time, through the right channel.

Most business owners make two mistakes:

1. They ask verbally at checkout — the customer is distracted, 
   in a hurry, and will forget within 5 minutes.

2. They don't ask at all — they just hope reviews will come naturally.

The businesses that get 15-25 new reviews per month do one thing 
differently: they automate the ask.

Here's the system:

1. Collect customer contact info at booking or checkout (phone number 
   is best — SMS has 45% open rates vs email's 20%)

2. Send a review request 30-60 minutes after service — this is the 
   "afterglow window" where the positive experience is still fresh

3. Include a direct Google Review link — one tap takes them straight 
   to the review form. No searching for your business name.

4. Make it personal — use their name, mention the service they had

5. Respond to every review — businesses that respond to 100% of 
   reviews see a 12% rating increase over 6 months

WhatsApp has the highest conversion rate (4.5%) because of its 98% 
open rate. If your customers use WhatsApp, send review requests there.

entire process via SMS, email, and WhatsApp — including the AI reply 
generator for responses. But even without a tool, just setting up a 
system to ask every customer consistently will 3x your reviews.

Happy to answer specific questions about what works for different 
industries.
```

## Answer 3: "How to automate Google review requests?"

```
Automating Google review requests is surprisingly simple once you 
know the components:

1. A direct Google Review link — this is a URL that takes customers 
   straight to your review form. Every verified Google Business 
   Profile has one.

2. A message template — SMS, email, or WhatsApp message that includes 
   the link. Should be short, personal, and sent at the right time.

3. A delivery system — software that sends the message automatically 
   after each customer interaction.

The actual setup:

Step 1: Get your Google Review link
Go to your Google Business Profile → "Ask for reviews" → copy the 
shareable link. This link is permanent and works for all customers.

Step 2: Create your template
Keep it simple: "Hey [Name]! Thanks for visiting [Business] today. 
Got 30 seconds? Leave us a Google review: [Link]"

Step 3: Set up automation
You can use:
- A spreadsheet + manual send (free but time-consuming)
- Email marketing tools like Mailchimp (works for email only)
- A dedicated review request tool (best option)

I built ReviewPing (reviewping.pro) to handle this in 2 minutes — 
import your customer list, choose SMS/Email/WhatsApp, set timing, 
and it runs on autopilot. But there are other options too.

The most important thing is timing. Send within 30-60 minutes after 
service. After 24 hours, conversion drops by 80%. The businesses 
that nail this one variable get 3x more reviews than those that don't.
```

## Answer 5: "What is the best way to ask customers for Google reviews?"

```
The best way depends on your business type, but here's what works 
based on data from 2,400+ businesses:

Channel Rankings:

1. WhatsApp — 98% open rate, 4.5% review completion rate
   Best for: Salons, international businesses, younger customers

2. SMS — 45% open rate (within 1 hour), 3.6% review completion
   Best for: Restaurants, auto repair, home services, retail

3. Email — 20% open rate (within 24 hours), 2.4% review completion
   Best for: Dental clinics, medical practices, professional services

Timing:

- Restaurants & cafes: 30 minutes after meal
- Salons & spas: 30-60 minutes after service
- Clinics: 2-4 hours after appointment
- Home services: 1 hour after job completion
- E-commerce: 24 hours after delivery

The Template:

"Hey [Name]! Thanks for visiting [Business] today. Got 30 seconds? 
Leave a Google review here: [Link]"

That's it. Short, personal, direct. The link should go straight to 
your Google review form — no searching, no logging in.

What NOT to do:
- Don't offer discounts in exchange for reviews (violates Google policy)
- Don't ask verbally at the counter (5% conversion — awful)
- Don't send generic messages (use their name for 30% higher conversion)
- Don't wait more than 24 hours (conversion drops 80%)

I built ReviewPing (reviewping.pro) to automate this whole process 
with the optimal timing and channel selection. But even applying
```
