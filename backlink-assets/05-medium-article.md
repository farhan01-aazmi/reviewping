## Body

### The $7,600 Mistake

Two years ago, I signed up for Podium. Like most small business owners, I'd heard it was the best way to get Google reviews. My dental practice needed more reviews, so I handed over my credit card.

What I didn't expect was the bill.

First year total: $7,600.
- $400/month subscription: $4,800
- Setup fee: $2,000
- SMS overages: ~$800

I was paying $633 per month for text messages with a link.

### The Moment I Realised

One evening, I sat down and made a list of every Podium feature I actually used:

1. SMS review requests ✅
2. Everything else ❌

That's it. I was paying for AI chatbots, team inboxes, payment processing, social media monitoring, and enterprise compliance — and using exactly none of it.

I asked 50 other small business owners at a local networking event. Forty-three of them had the same experience. They signed up for review requests and ended up with an expensive platform doing 20 things they didn't need.

### The WhatsApp Gap

Then I noticed something. About 30% of my customers preferred WhatsApp over SMS. They'd text me on WhatsApp to book appointments or ask questions. But Podium didn't support WhatsApp review requests.

Every customer who preferred WhatsApp was a review I'd never get.

I searched for a tool that was:
- Affordable ($30-50/month range)
- Did SMS, email, AND WhatsApp
- No setup fees
- No long-term contracts
- Setup in minutes, not weeks

I couldn't find one. So I built it.

### Building ReviewPing

I'm not a developer by trade. I'm a business owner who learned enough code to solve his own problem. ReviewPing started as a weekend project — a simple tool that sent SMS messages with a Google Review link after each appointment.

The first version was ugly. But it worked. Within a month, I was getting 3x more reviews than I ever got with Podium.

I showed it to a few other local business owners. They wanted access. So I cleaned it up, added email and WhatsApp support, built an AI reply generator (so no one has to stare at a bad review wondering what to say), and launched it properly.

### What ReviewPing Does

It does one thing: automate the process of asking customers for reviews.

1. Import your customer list (CSV upload, takes 30 seconds)
2. Choose your channel — SMS, Email, or WhatsApp
3. Pick a template or write your own
4. Set your timing — send requests 30 minutes after service
5. Watch the reviews come in automatically
6. Respond to every review using the AI generator

That's it. No bloat. No modules you'll never open. No features you're paying for but not using.

### What I Learned

**1. Most SaaS products are built for enterprises, not small businesses.**

Enterprise features are profitable. Simple features for small businesses aren't. That's why Podium costs $400/month — they need to pay for the enterprise sales team, the 3-week onboarding process, and the dedicated account managers.

But most small businesses don't need enterprise software. They need something that works, is affordable, and takes 5 minutes to set up.

**2. WhatsApp is massively underrated for business communication.**

WhatsApp has 2 billion users and 98% open rates. Yet most review request platforms ignore it entirely. For businesses serving international customers, younger demographics, or markets where WhatsApp is dominant (India, Brazil, Mexico, much of Europe), this is a huge missed opportunity.

**3. Hidden fees are a business model.**

Podium's $400/month is just the starting point. Setup fees, overage charges, add-on modules — by the time you've added everything you need, the real cost is 50-100% higher than the advertised price.

Transparent pricing isn't just ethical — it's a competitive advantage.
